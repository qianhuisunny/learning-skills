# Module 2.2 — Commercial and financial inputs for contract preparation

**Target learner:** EY professionals and public-sector project teams involved in infrastructure procurement
**Duration target:** ~20 min
**Assessment strategy:** 2 scenario MCQs (one per objective, from outline)

| Objective | Assessment | Practice | Presentation |
|-----------|------------|----------|--------------|
| **Identify** key principles and best practices in exclusive contract negotiation and transaction support | Scenario MCQ: given a procurement stage, determine what the team should focus on first (outline Q1) | Knowledge-check MCQ: sequence the stages of the procurement process leading to negotiation (outline Q2) | **Slide 2 (L03):** Procurement process + entry to negotiation. Left: procurement lifecycle flowchart (Market Engagement → RFQ → RFP → Negotiations) with negotiation highlighted. Right: what triggers entry (preferred proponent selected), what it means (exclusive right to negotiate terms). Cognitive purpose: context + concept in one visual — learner sees WHERE and WHAT simultaneously. **Slide 3 (L03):** What negotiation finalizes + how inputs map to close. Left: two-close structure (Commercial Close: PA, construction contract, services contract, letters of credit, performance security. Financial Close: adapted PA, aligned docs, price adjustment). Right: diagram showing commercial inputs → commercial close docs, financial inputs → financial close docs. Key callout: timing, payment structures, alignment. Cognitive purpose: learner sees END STATE and INPUT→OUTPUT mapping together. |
| **Identify** commercial and financial inputs required for contract preparation | Scenario MCQ: given incomplete inputs, determine what to obtain next (outline Q3) | Knowledge-check MCQ: classify inputs by category — commercial vs financial (outline Q4) | **Slide 4 (L12 menu hub):** Two bars — "Commercial inputs" and "Financial inputs." **Commercial popup:** What (contract types, key terms & conditions per 6.1+6.2). Where from (procurement/transaction development). Who (Technical, Financial, Legal Advisors, Procurement Authority). Why (shape contractual structure). **Financial popup:** What (Financial Plan/Offering, Funding Plan, Financial Modelling, Price Forms). Where from (financial submission requirements). Who (Financial Advisors). Why (financial suitability, VFM, pricing, funding). Cognitive purpose: side-by-side contrast forces learner to notice the boundary between commercial and financial. **Slide 5 (L03):** How inputs map to close documents. Left: commercial inputs flowing into commercial close docs. Right: financial inputs flowing into financial close docs. Bridges Obj 1 (close structure) with Obj 2 (what the inputs are). |
