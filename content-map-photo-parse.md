### 3.2 Identify Performance Monitoring and Reporting Mechanisms to Ensure Compliance

**Lesson objective**

Identify performance monitoring and reporting mechanisms to ensure compliance.

- Focus of the row: what mechanisms are used to monitor performance, report results, and support compliance once the contract is in delivery.

**Assessment**

`(Number)` Multiple-choice questions on performance monitoring and reporting mechanisms used to ensure compliance.

- Scenario-based MCQs that ask learners to identify the most appropriate monitoring or reporting mechanism for a contract-management situation.
- Question focus: KPIs, monitoring plans, data collection, review meetings, reporting mechanisms, feedback loops, and corrective action when performance deviates from obligations.

**Practice and feedback**

Practice scenario continued: the contract is now in delivery, and the project team needs to check whether the contractor is meeting contractual obligations. Which mechanisms should they put in place first?

- Learners identify which mechanisms help the team monitor compliance in a structured way.
- Practice focus:
  - Defining KPIs
  - Creating a monitoring plan
  - Collecting performance data
  - Running review meetings
  - Using reporting outputs to decide corrective action
- Feedback explains why each mechanism does or does not support compliance monitoring.

**Presentation content**

Slide 1 - Why performance monitoring and reporting matter  
Source: `Source document.pptx`, pp. 45, 47-49

- Performance monitoring and reporting are essential components of contract management that help ensure alignment with project objectives.
- They allow stakeholders to track progress and outcomes, make informed decisions, and address issues proactively.
- Where deviations are identified, they should be addressed promptly to mitigate risks.
- Performance data and stakeholder feedback also support adjustments, continuous improvement, and final evaluation against objectives.

Slide 2 - Core monitoring mechanisms, 3-column layout  
Source: `Source document.pptx`, pp. 47-48

- Column 1: KPIs - establish measurable KPIs aligned with project objectives. The source says these should cover quality, time, cost, and stakeholder satisfaction.
- Column 2: Monitoring plan - create a structured plan that sets out data collection methods, frequency of reporting, and responsible parties.
- Column 3: Data collection and analysis - collect performance data consistently and analyse it to identify trends, variances, and areas needing attention.

Slide 3 - Reporting and review mechanisms  
Source: `Source document.pptx`, pp. 48-49

- Conduct performance review meetings with stakeholders to discuss progress against KPIs, challenges faced, and corrective actions needed.
- Use clear reporting mechanisms/formats for stakeholders.
- The source notes that charts and graphs can enhance understanding of performance data.
- Include a feedback loop so reporting informs process refinement and future performance improvement.

Slide 4 - Change management and variations  
Source: `Source document.pptx`, pp. 50-54

- What drives change:
  - Regulatory changes
  - Stakeholder requests
  - Technological advancements
  - Environmental factors
  - Budget constraints
- Core process steps:
  - Change identification
  - Change request submission
  - Impact analysis
  - Review and approval
  - Implementation
  - Monitoring and evaluation
- What supports the process:
  - Change management plans
  - Change Control Boards (CCB)
  - Stakeholder engagement strategies
  - Project management software
